Research Interests

  • Mathematical Oncology & Immunology: leukemia modeling and treatment optimization; CAR-T cell therapy; tumor dynamics and evolution of phenotypic heterogeneity; tumor-immune interactions; patient-specific treatments and virtual trials.
  • Mathematical Ecology: ecological resilience; Lotka-Volterra systems; biological invasions; reaction-diffusion models; structured populations.
  • Dynamical Systems & Differential Equations: qualitative theory of ODEs (basins of attraction, invariant manifolds, bifurcations); compartmental models (ODEs and PDEs); parameter estimation methods; asymptotic analysis and numerical simulation.
