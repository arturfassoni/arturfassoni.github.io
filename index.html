Research Interests

Mathematical Biology - mathematical models for biological phenomena

  • Mathematical Oncology: treatment optimization; tumor-immune interactions; tumor microenvironment; patient-specific treatments; leukemia treatment; CAR-T cell therapy; virtual trials.
  • Mathematical Ecology: resilience analysis for population dynamics models; Lotka-Volterra systems; biological invasions; reaction-diffusion equations in ecology; structured populations.
  • Differential Equations: qualitative theory of ODEs (basins of attractions, invariant manifolds, bifurcations); parameter estimation methods; asymptotic analysis and numerical simulation of PDEs.
